# mdview Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

mdview is a Markdown viewer for iPhone and iPad, published by Kevin Gould / Par72 Labs, LLC. This policy covers the mdview app (bundle ID `us.par72.mdview`).

## Summary

mdview does not collect any data. It has no network access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The files you open never leave your device.

## Data we collect

None. mdview does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information or usage data. There is no server to send it to.

## Your files

mdview reads the Markdown and text files you choose to open, through the iOS document browser, the Files app, the share sheet, Open In, or drag and drop. The app only reads files you open. It shows them on screen and does not upload, copy to any server, or modify them. Images referenced by a document are shown only when they are local files next to the document. Web images (`http` or `https`) are never downloaded; they appear as a link that opens in Safari only if you tap it.

## Network access

mdview does not connect to the internet. It contains no networking code, no third-party SDKs, no crash reporters, and no analytics.

## Settings stored on your device

mdview stores two preferences in the app's on-device settings:

- whether documents open in Rendered or Source view
- whether Obsidian extras are on

iOS also keeps its own list of recently opened documents for the document browser. These stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app (the Recents list is managed by iOS).

## Tracking

mdview does not track you across apps or websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

## Children

mdview collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
